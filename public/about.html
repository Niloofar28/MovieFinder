Our story

Discretion, judgement and a good eye

Horizon Properties began as a two-person practice advising architects and their clients on the sale of significant houses. That origin still shapes how we work: we value the building as much as the transaction, and we would rather lose a listing than misrepresent one.

Today the firm covers twelve prime markets with a team of four advisors. We keep our portfolio deliberately small so that each property receives a considered campaign — real photography, a written story, and a buyer list built person by person rather than bought by the thousand.
